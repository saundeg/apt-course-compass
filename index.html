Welcome to the APTL Course Compass

This diagnostic helps your course team understand where you currently are in integrating AI into your curriculum, and what to prioritise next. It covers six key dimensions of course-level AI maturity and takes approximately 20-30 minutes to complete as a team discussion.

The tool generates a personalised report with a maturity profile, priority areas, and specific suggested actions linked to Westminster resources and the APTL course packages.

How to use this tool: Work through each dimension as a team - ideally with your course leader facilitating a brief discussion before selecting the level that best describes where your course currently sits. Be honest; the value is in accurate diagnosis, not in selecting the most flattering option.
